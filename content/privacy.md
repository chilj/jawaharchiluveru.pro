This site is a personal portfolio. It doesn't sell data, show ads or use your information for anything beyond understanding which content is useful.

## What's collected

If analytics is on and you've allowed it (visitors in Europe are asked first), Microsoft Clarity and Google Analytics record anonymous usage: pages viewed, clicks and taps, scrolling, device type and approximate location. Clarity may record sessions so I can see how the page is used. Text you type is masked.

Visitors with **Do Not Track** or **Global Privacy Control** turned on in their browser aren't tracked at all.

## Your choices

Use **Cookie settings** at the bottom of any page to accept or decline analytics cookies, or to change your mind later.
